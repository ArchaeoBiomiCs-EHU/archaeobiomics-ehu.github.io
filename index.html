About ArchaeoBiomiCs

We are a research group at the University of the Basque Country, working at the intersection of ancient genomics, archaeology and forensic genetics.

We work together with anthropologists, archaeologists and historians to understand past societies and contribute to the identification of human remains.

We belong to the Grupo Consolidado Microfluidics & Biomics cluster UPV/EHU.
