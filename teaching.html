Teaching

Master of
Forensic Analysis

The programme offers multidisciplinary training in the methods used to examine forensic evidence, bringing together genetics, anthropology, microbiology and physical and chemical analysis.

Practical training and a master’s dissertation connect scientific knowledge with professional and research contexts.

Our group is responsible for the four courses on Forensic Genetics within the Master's programme:

  • Introduction to Forensic Genetics. (3 ECTS)
  • Techniques of analysis in Forensic Genetics. (3 ECTS)
  • Applications of Forensic Genetics in the identification of missing people and kinship. (3 ECTS)
  • Applications of Forensic Genetics in criminalistics. (3 ECTS)
